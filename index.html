How to Paste Your Signature

Gmail
  1. Click Copy Signature on your preferred design above.
  2. In Gmail, click Settings (cog) → See all settings.
  3. Under the General tab, scroll down to Signature.
  4. Click Create new, then press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V) into the box.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
Apple Mail (macOS)
  1. Click Copy Signature above.
  2. Open Mail → Settings → Signatures.
  3. Add a signature, click in the preview box, and press Cmd+V.
  4. Crucial: Uncheck "Always match my default message font" so your custom layout and sizes stay exact.
Outlook / Webmail
  1. Click Copy Signature above.
  2. In Outlook, go to Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature.
  3. Click New signature and paste directly into the editor box.
  4. Set as default for new messages and replies, then click Save.
