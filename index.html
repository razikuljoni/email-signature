How to Paste Your Signature

1
Copy Signature

Click Copy Signature on your preferred card above. This loads rendered HTML & formatting directly into your clipboard.

2
Open Gmail Settings

In Gmail, click the cog icon in top-right → See all settings. Under the General tab, scroll down to Signature.

3
Paste & Save

Click + Create new, name your signature, click inside the editor box, and press Cmd + V (or Ctrl + V). Scroll down and click Save Changes.

1
Copy Signature

Click Copy Signature above.

2
Mail Preferences

In macOS Mail, choose Mail → Settings → Signatures. Select your account and click +.

3
Paste & Crucial Checkbox

Click into the signature preview area and press Cmd + V. Important: Uncheck "Always match my default message font" at the bottom so your styles remain preserved.

1
Copy Signature

Click Copy Signature on your design.

2
Outlook Web Settings

Click the Settings gear icon → Mail → Compose and reply.

3
Paste & Set Default

Click + New signature, paste with Ctrl + V or Cmd + V, assign as default for new messages, and click Save.

1
Copy Signature

Click Copy Signature on any card.

2
Signature Manager

In Outlook desktop app, click File → Options → Mail → Signatures (or Outlook → Settings → Signatures on Mac).

3
Paste into Box

Click New, paste directly into the bottom edit box, set as default for new emails, and click OK.
